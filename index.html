What stage do you typically invest in?

We focus on seed through Series B, partnering with founders who have early traction and a clear vision for scale. We also selectively invest in later rounds to support our existing portfolio companies.

How involved are you with portfolio companies?

We roll up our sleeves and work alongside founders—without overstepping. From hiring to go-to-market strategy, we provide hands-on support while respecting your vision and autonomy.

What makes Castle Ventures different?

We're operators first, investors second. Our team has built and scaled companies, so we understand the challenges founders face—and we've built a community to help navigate them together.

How do I get in touch?

Send us your pitch deck and a short intro to hello@castlevelventures.com. We review every submission and aim to respond within two weeks.
